# Keyboard sounds

`keys/k01.wav … k32.wav` are the 32 single key presses of **Keyboard Soundpack #1** by unicaegames,
from [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/keyboard-soundpack-1-typing-and-single-keystrokes),
released under CC0 (public domain). Recorded on a Cherry KC 1000 with a Shure SM7B.
The site pitches them down and filters them at playback to make them rounder.
